How to read these: the shaded area is the median snow water equivalent (SWE) for each day over the station's period of record (POR), excluding the current year. The blue line is the current water year (Oct 1 – Sep 30). The percentage compares the latest reading with the median for that day. More about the data and how to read the charts.
