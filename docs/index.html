How to read these: the shaded area is the median snow water equivalent (SWE) for each day over the station's period of record (POR), excluding the current year. The blue line is the current water year (Oct 1 – Sep 30). The percentage compares the latest reading with the median for that day. Hover over a chart to read values for any date, and use “Compare with past years” to see how earlier seasons stood on today’s date. More about the data and how to read the charts.

Data: USDA NRCS National Water and Climate Center. Provisional data, subject to revision.
