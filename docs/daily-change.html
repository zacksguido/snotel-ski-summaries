Daily Snow Change

Change in snow water equivalent (SWE), in inches, over the previous 1, 3, 5, and 7 days, ending on each station's latest reading. Click a column heading to sort.
▲ +1.2 gain (usually new snow) ▼ −0.8 loss (usually melt) — no reading that day Dates in italics are older than the newest reading
Changes compare each station's latest reading with its reading 1, 3, 5, and 7 days earlier. SWE measures the water in the snowpack, so settling alone doesn't change it; a gain is roughly new snow's water content, not its depth. Data: USDA NRCS National Water and Climate Center.
